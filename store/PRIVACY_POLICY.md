# Privacy Policy — Taka Counter

**Effective date:** 6 November 2026
**App:** Taka Counter (`com.devbysharif.takacounter`)
**Developer:** DevBySharif
**Contact:** hello@takacounter.app

## The short version

Taka Counter does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
The app works fully offline. Your counts, notes, and settings stay in the app's
local storage on your own device.

## What the app stores

Taka Counter saves the following on your device:

- the cash counts you create, including titles, dates, amounts, notes, and
  note/coin breakdowns;
- your preferences, such as theme, haptics, and delete confirmation;
- the identifier of the count that was last open.

This information is written to the app's private local storage
(`SharedPreferences`). It is not uploaded to us or to any third party. The app
has no server, no user account, and no cloud sync.

Android cloud backup is disabled for this app (`allowBackup="false"`), so the
app's data is not included in Google's device backup.

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-up, email address, or profile.
- No advertising, ad SDKs, or tracking.
- No analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permission.
- No internet permission. The app cannot send data anywhere on its own.
- No selling or sharing of personal data.

## Exports and sharing

Taka Counter can create files only when you ask it to:

- an Excel workbook (`.xlsx`), a JSON backup, or a CSV file;
- an A5 PDF voucher;
- a plain-text summary of a count.

These files are written to temporary app storage and handed to the Android
share sheet or the system file picker. You choose the destination — for
example Google Drive, email, WhatsApp, or a printer. Once a file leaves the
app, the receiving app or service handles it under its own privacy policy. We
never receive a copy.

Importing a file uses Android's system file picker. The chosen file is read
only to restore the data you selected.

## Children's privacy

Taka Counter is a financial utility for adults and is not directed to children
under 13. The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone,
including children.

## Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox: other apps cannot read Taka
Counter's private storage without root access or a device-level compromise.
Because nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit to intercept. Keep
your device lock enabled and back up your device as you would for any important
local data.

## Your choices and deletion

- Delete individual entries or whole counts inside the app at any time.
- Clear all app data from Android Settings → Apps → Taka Counter → Storage.
- Uninstalling the app removes its local data from the device.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a
server. If you have a question about deletion, contact us at the address below.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the
effective date revised before the change ships. Material changes will also be
noted in the Play Store release notes.
