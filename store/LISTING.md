### Full description

```
Taka Counter is a calm, private cash-counting app for Bangladesh — a khata for money in and out, plus a counting mat for notes and coins.

COUNT CASH YOUR WAY
• Add cash, credit, and debit entries by typing the amount and the number of notes.
• Quick amount chips and +/− steppers are there when you want them.
• Every count is saved on your phone, ready when you need it.

NOTES AND COINS, ONE TOTAL
• Count ৳1 to ৳1,000 banknotes and ৳1, ৳2, and ৳5 coins.
• Tap to add one, hold for ×10, or type a quantity for any denomination.
• The tray total goes into your count as one amount — and the full breakdown stays with it.
• Reopen any entry to see or edit exactly how many of each note and coin you counted.

SHARE A CLEAN VOUCHER
• Turn a count into an A5 PDF cash-count voucher with a voucher number, entry table, total in words, and signature lines.
• Print it or send it on WhatsApp, email, or Drive.

BACKUP AND EXPORT
• Export every count to a real Excel (.xlsx) workbook, with a notes-and-coins column.
• Save a JSON backup and restore it later; import Excel, CSV, or a backup file.
• Send exported files to Google Drive from the Android share sheet.

MADE TO FEEL RIGHT
• Warm cream light mode and a deep forest dark mode.
• Bengali note names, Bengali numerals on the note artwork, and lakh/crore grouping (৳1,93,130).
• Gentle haptics, clean typography, and a focused layout.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no cloud.
• Works fully offline. Your counts stay in local storage on your device.
• The app requests no internet, location, contacts, camera, or storage permission.

Whether you run a shop, manage a household fund, or count the day's collection, Taka Counter keeps every taka clear.

Made with care for Bangladesh by DevBySharif.
```
